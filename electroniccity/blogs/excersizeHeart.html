Exercise & Heart Health

Safe and effective workouts to strengthen your cardiovascular system and improve overall fitness.

Contact Us Book Appointment

Why Exercise is Essential for Heart Health

Regular physical activity is one of the most effective ways to maintain a healthy heart. Exercise strengthens your heart muscle, improves blood circulation, and helps manage risk factors for heart disease. At Raksha Hospital, we emphasize the importance of safe, structured exercise programs tailored to individual health needs.

Three Pillars of Heart-Healthy Exercise

🏃‍♂️ Aerobic Exercise (Cardio)

Aerobic exercises increase your heart rate and breathing, strengthening your heart and lungs. These activities improve cardiovascular endurance and help burn calories.

  • Walking: Start with 30 minutes daily, gradually increasing pace and duration
  • Cycling: Low-impact option that's easy on joints
  • Swimming: Full-body workout that's gentle on the heart
  • Dancing: Fun way to get cardio while improving coordination

Recommended: 150 minutes of moderate-intensity or 75 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic activity per week.

💪 Strength Training

Building muscle mass helps your body work more efficiently and can improve heart health by reducing body fat and improving metabolism.

  • Bodyweight exercises: Push-ups, squats, lunges, planks
  • Resistance bands: Portable and safe for beginners
  • Light weights: Dumbbells or kettlebells for progressive resistance
  • Yoga: Improves flexibility and core strength

Recommended: 2-3 sessions per week, focusing on major muscle groups.

🧘‍♀️ Flexibility & Balance

Flexibility exercises improve range of motion and reduce injury risk, while balance exercises help prevent falls, especially important as we age.

  • Stretching: Gentle stretches for major muscle groups
  • Tai Chi: Slow, controlled movements that improve balance
  • Pilates: Core strengthening with focus on control and precision
  • Balance exercises: Standing on one leg, heel-to-toe walking

Recommended: 2-3 sessions per week, holding stretches for 15-30 seconds.

Safe Exercise Guidelines for Heart Health

🚨 Before Starting

  • Consult your doctor, especially if you have heart conditions
  • Start slowly and gradually increase intensity
  • Warm up for 5-10 minutes before exercise
  • Stay hydrated throughout your workout

✅ During Exercise

  • Monitor your heart rate and breathing
  • Stop if you experience chest pain or dizziness
  • Maintain proper form to prevent injury
  • Listen to your body's signals

🔄 After Exercise

  • Cool down with gentle stretching
  • Gradually reduce intensity
  • Rehydrate and refuel appropriately
  • Monitor for any unusual symptoms

When to Stop Exercising

While exercise is generally safe and beneficial, it's important to recognize warning signs that indicate you should stop exercising and seek medical attention:

🚨 Stop Immediately

  • Chest pain or pressure
  • Severe shortness of breath
  • Dizziness or lightheadedness
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Nausea or vomiting

⚠️ Slow Down

  • Mild shortness of breath
  • Fatigue that doesn't improve
  • Muscle or joint pain
  • Excessive sweating
  • Feeling unusually tired
