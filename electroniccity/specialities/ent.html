Leading ENT Department in Electronic City

Our ENT department at Raksha Hospital provides comprehensive care for all ear, nose, throat, and head-neck conditions. The team brings expertise in otology, rhinology, laryngology, and head-neck surgery. We are equipped with advanced ENT equipment and recognized as one of the leading ENT centers in Electronic City, Bengaluru.

ENT Conditions We Treat

  • Hearing loss and ear infections
  • Sinus disorders and nasal polyps
  • Voice disorders and swallowing problems
  • Throat infections and tonsillitis
  • Head and neck tumors

ENT Diagnostics & Procedures

  • Audiometry and hearing tests
  • Nasal endoscopy and sinus imaging
  • Laryngoscopy and voice analysis
  • CT and MRI for head-neck evaluation
  • Allergy testing for ENT conditions

ENT Treatments & Surgeries

  • Endoscopic sinus surgery
  • Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy
  • Hearing aid fitting and cochlear implants
  • Voice therapy and laryngeal surgery
  • Head and neck cancer treatment
