Book your appointment

Tell us who you would like to see and when. Your request goes straight to our front desk on WhatsApp and we confirm within 24 hours.

Appointment request

Only your name is required — add as much detail as you like.

Opens WhatsApp with your request ready to send. Prefer to talk? Call +91 94940 60671.

What happens next?

  1. Send your request

    Fill in the form and tap “Send on WhatsApp”. Your request reaches our front desk directly.

  2. We confirm your slot

    Our team replies within 24 hours to confirm the doctor, date and time.

  3. Visit the hospital

    Arrive 15 minutes early with your ID, reports and current medications.

Need immediate help?

For emergencies, do not wait for a reply — call us now.

  • List your current medications
  • Note down your symptoms
  • Carry previous reports and prescriptions
  • Bring a family member if needed
