Why Raksha

Why families choose Raksha Hospital

Raksha Multi Speciality Hospital offers a comprehensive range of medical services across multiple specialities. With modern technology and a team of highly skilled doctors, we provide care in fields such as cardiology, orthopaedics, neurology and emergency medicine.

About Electronic CityAbout Kudlu

  • Experienced specialists

    Qualified, experienced consultants across medical and surgical specialities.

  • 24×7 emergency team

    Emergency doctors and staff ready for immediate care, round the clock.

  • Modern facilities

    ICU with ventilators, operation theatres, an in-house lab and imaging.

  • Patient-centred care

    Personalised treatment plans, explained clearly and delivered with compassion.

  • Integrated services

    Close coordination between departments for faster diagnosis and treatment.

  • Affordable, transparent care
