General Surgery is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating a wide range of surgical conditions affecting various parts of the body, including the abdomen, skin, soft tissues, and digestive system.

General surgeons perform procedures such as appendectomies, hernia repairs, gallbladder removals, and trauma surgery. They specialize in both traditional open surgeries and minimally invasive laparoscopic techniques.

Early diagnosis, advanced surgical techniques, and post-operative care are crucial for ensuring successful recovery and optimal patient outcomes.
