Urology is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to the urinary tract and male reproductive system. It covers disorders affecting the kidneys, bladder, urethra, and male reproductive organs.

Urologists treat conditions such as urinary tract infections, kidney stones, incontinence, prostate disorders, and urologic cancers. They also perform surgical procedures like kidney stone removal, prostate surgery, and bladder reconstruction.

Early detection and proper management are crucial for maintaining urological health. Regular check-ups and timely intervention can significantly improve patient outcomes.
