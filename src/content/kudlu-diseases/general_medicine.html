General Medicine is a specialized branch of healthcare that focuses on diagnosing, treating, and preventing a wide range of medical conditions affecting adults. It includes managing chronic diseases, acute illnesses, and preventive healthcare.

Physicians in General Medicine treat conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, respiratory infections, digestive disorders, and autoimmune diseases. They provide comprehensive care through medical management, lifestyle modifications, and preventive screenings.

Early diagnosis, regular health check-ups, and effective medical management are crucial for maintaining overall well-being and preventing complications.
