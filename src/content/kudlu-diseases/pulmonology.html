Pulmonology is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to the respiratory system. It includes diseases affecting the lungs, airways, and respiratory muscles.

Pulmonologists treat conditions such as asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), pneumonia, lung fibrosis, and sleep apnea. They provide specialized treatments, including oxygen therapy, bronchoscopy, and pulmonary rehabilitation.

Early detection, personalized treatment plans, and advanced respiratory therapies are crucial for improving lung health and overall well-being.
