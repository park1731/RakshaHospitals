Ophthalmology is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to the eyes and vision. It covers disorders such as cataracts, glaucoma, refractive errors, and retinal diseases.

Ophthalmologists treat conditions such as vision impairment, eye infections, diabetic retinopathy, and corneal diseases. They also perform procedures like LASIK, cataract surgery, and retinal treatments.

Early diagnosis and proper eye care are crucial for maintaining vision health. Regular check-ups and timely treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes.
