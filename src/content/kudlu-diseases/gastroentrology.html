Gastroenterology is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to the digestive system. It includes diseases affecting the stomach, intestines, liver, pancreas, and gallbladder.

Gastroenterologists treat conditions such as acid reflux, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), liver disease, ulcers, and inflammatory bowel disease (IBD). They provide specialized treatments, including endoscopy, colonoscopy, and liver function management.

Early detection, personalized treatment plans, and advanced digestive health management are crucial for improving overall well-being.
