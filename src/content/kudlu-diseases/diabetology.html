Diabetology is the branch of medicine dedicated to the diagnosis, treatment and long-term management of diabetes and related metabolic conditions. Diabetes affects how the body regulates blood sugar and, if it is not controlled, can damage the heart, kidneys, eyes, nerves and blood vessels over time.

Diabetologists care for people with type 1 diabetes, type 2 diabetes, gestational diabetes and prediabetes. Care combines regular monitoring, medication or insulin where needed, and practical guidance on diet, activity and lifestyle.

Early diagnosis and steady blood-sugar control are the keys to preventing complications. Regular check-ups help you stay ahead of diabetes.
