Leading Nephrology Department in Electronic City

Our Nephrology department at Raksha Hospital provides comprehensive care for all kidney and renal conditions. The team brings expertise in chronic kidney disease management, dialysis services, transplant evaluation, and hypertension management. We are equipped with modern dialysis facilities and recognized as one of the leading kidney care centers in Electronic City, Bengaluru.

Kidney Conditions We Treat

  • Chronic kidney disease and acute kidney injury
  • End-stage renal disease requiring dialysis
  • Kidney stones and urinary tract disorders
  • Hypertension related to kidney disease
  • Electrolyte and acid-base disorders

Nephrology Diagnostics

  • Kidney function tests and creatinine clearance
  • Urinalysis and proteinuria assessment
  • Kidney biopsy and histopathology
  • Ultrasound and CT imaging of kidneys
  • Electrolyte and mineral metabolism tests

Nephrology Treatments & Services

  • Hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis
  • Kidney transplant evaluation and preparation
  • Chronic kidney disease management
  • Hypertension and diabetes management
  • Nutritional counseling for kidney patients
