Advanced ICU with Ventilator Facility in Electronic City

Our ICU at Raksha Hospital provides advanced intensive care with state-of-the-art ventilator facilities and 24/7 monitoring. The team brings expertise in critical care medicine, emergency response, and life support systems. We are equipped with modern ICU equipment and staffed by expert intensivists and critical care nurses.

Critical Conditions We Manage

  • Respiratory failure requiring mechanical ventilation
  • Post-operative critical care and monitoring
  • Sepsis and multi-organ dysfunction
  • Cardiac emergencies and hemodynamic instability
  • Neurological emergencies and coma management

ICU Monitoring & Equipment

  • Advanced mechanical ventilators and BIPAP
  • Continuous cardiac and hemodynamic monitoring
  • Central venous pressure and arterial line monitoring
  • Pulse oximetry and capnography
  • Defibrillators and emergency resuscitation equipment

ICU Services & Procedures

  • Mechanical ventilation and respiratory support
  • Central line insertion and invasive monitoring
  • Emergency intubation and airway management
  • Hemodynamic support and vasopressor therapy
  • 24/7 critical care nursing and physician coverage
