Leading Cardiology Department in Electronic City

Our Cardiology department at Raksha Hospital provides comprehensive care for all heart and vascular conditions. The team brings expertise across preventive cardiology, interventional cardiology, electrophysiology, and cardiac imaging, supported by a 24×7 cath lab and ICU. We are recognized as one of the best cardiology hospitals in Electronic City, Bengaluru.

Conditions We Treat

  • Coronary artery disease and acute coronary syndromes
  • Arrhythmias including atrial fibrillation and SVT
  • Heart failure and cardiomyopathies
  • Valvular heart disease
  • Hypertension, dyslipidemia, and metabolic risk

Diagnostics

  • ECG, Holter, and event monitoring
  • 2D/3D Echocardiography and stress echo
  • CT coronary angiography
  • Cardiac MRI (as indicated via partner centers)
  • Invasive coronary angiography

Treatments and Procedures

  • Angioplasty and stenting
  • Pacemaker and ICD implantation
  • Electrophysiology studies and ablation
  • Structural interventions including TAVR (as indicated)
  • Cardiac rehab and secondary prevention
