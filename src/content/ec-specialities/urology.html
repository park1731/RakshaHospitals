Leading Urology Department in Electronic City

Our Urology team treats stones, infections, prostate enlargement, cancers and reconstructive problems using the latest endoscopic and minimally invasive approaches. We are recognized as one of the best urology departments in Electronic City, Bengaluru.

Conditions We Treat

  • Kidney and ureteric stones
  • Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH)
  • Urinary tract infections
  • Male infertility and andrology
  • Urologic cancers

Diagnostics

  • Ultrasound KUB and CT urography
  • Uroflowmetry and urodynamic studies
  • Cystoscopy and biopsy

Treatments

  • URS, RIRS and PCNL for stones
  • Laser prostate surgery (HoLEP/TURP)
  • Endourology for strictures
  • Reconstructive urology
