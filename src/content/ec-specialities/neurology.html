Leading Neurology Department in Electronic City

Our Neurology services cover acute and chronic neurological illnesses with an emphasis on fast stroke management, epilepsy care, movement disorders, headaches, and neuro-rehabilitation. We are recognized as one of the best neurology departments in Electronic City, Bengaluru.

Conditions We Treat

  • Stroke and transient ischemic attacks
  • Epilepsy and seizures
  • Parkinson's disease and movement disorders
  • Migraines and chronic headaches
  • Peripheral neuropathy and myopathy

Diagnostics

  • EEG and video EEG
  • Nerve conduction studies and EMG
  • CT/MRI brain and spine
  • Vascular imaging (carotid/vertebral dopplers)

Treatments and Programs

  • Acute stroke protocols and thrombolysis
  • Anti-epileptic therapy and counseling
  • Botulinum toxin therapy for dystonia and spasticity
  • Headache clinics and preventive care
  • Neuro-rehabilitation and speech therapy
