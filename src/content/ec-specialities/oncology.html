Leading Oncology Department in Electronic City

Our Oncology department at Raksha Hospital provides comprehensive cancer care with advanced treatment options. The team brings expertise in medical oncology, surgical oncology, radiation therapy, and palliative care. We are equipped with modern cancer treatment facilities and recognized as one of the leading cancer care centers in Electronic City, Bengaluru.

Cancer Types We Treat

  • Breast cancer and gynecological cancers
  • Lung cancer and respiratory system tumors
  • Gastrointestinal cancers (stomach, colon, liver)
  • Blood cancers (leukemia, lymphoma, myeloma)
  • Head and neck cancers

Cancer Diagnostics

  • Advanced imaging (CT, MRI, PET scans)
  • Tumor biopsy and pathology analysis
  • Genetic testing and molecular diagnostics
  • Tumor markers and blood tests
  • Staging and treatment planning

Cancer Treatments & Procedures

  • Chemotherapy and targeted therapy
  • Radiation therapy and radiosurgery
  • Surgical oncology and tumor removal
  • Immunotherapy and precision medicine
  • Palliative care and pain management
