General Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment of everyday illnesses and long-term conditions.

Book AppointmentCall +91 80502 22777

General Medicine is a specialized branch of healthcare that focuses on diagnosing, treating, and preventing a wide range of medical conditions affecting adults. It includes managing chronic diseases, acute illnesses, and preventive healthcare.

Physicians in General Medicine treat conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, respiratory infections, digestive disorders, and autoimmune diseases. They provide comprehensive care through medical management, lifestyle modifications, and preventive screenings.

Early diagnosis, regular health check-ups, and effective medical management are crucial for maintaining overall well-being and preventing complications.

Why Raksha?

Our General Medicine team at Raksha Hospital comprises highly skilled physicians experienced in diagnosing and managing a variety of health conditions. At Raksha, we provide expert care in:

  • Hypertension & Diabetes Management
  • Respiratory & Infectious Disease Treatment
  • Gastrointestinal & Metabolic Disorder Care
  • Autoimmune & Chronic Disease Management
  • Comprehensive Preventive Health Screenings
  • Geriatric & Lifestyle Disease Care

Comprehensive General Medicine Treatments

Our general medicine department offers personalized treatment plans that include:

  • Medications for chronic & acute illnesses
  • Preventive health check-ups for early disease detection
  • Vaccination programs for infection prevention
  • Diet & lifestyle counseling for long-term health
  • Advanced diagnostic & laboratory tests for accurate assessments
  • Multidisciplinary approach for managing complex conditions

Maintaining overall health requires continuous monitoring and preventive care. At Raksha Hospital, we focus on early detection, expert medical management, and patient education to ensure long-term wellness and disease prevention.

Symptoms

  • Fever & body aches
  • Persistent fatigue
  • High blood pressure
  • Unexplained weight changes
  • Chronic cough & breathing issues
  • Digestive discomfort

Causes

  • Poor lifestyle & diet
  • Infections & viral illnesses
  • Genetic predisposition
  • Environmental factors
  • Chronic stress & lack of exercise
  • Age-related conditions

Treatment

  • Medications for disease management
  • Vaccination & immunization programs
  • Comprehensive health check-ups
  • Lifestyle & dietary interventions
  • Long-term disease monitoring
  • Rehabilitation & wellness programs

Care

  • Regular physician consultations
  • Maintaining a balanced diet
  • Engaging in regular exercise
  • Managing stress & mental well-being
  • Routine health screenings
  • Avoiding self-medication & monitoring symptoms
