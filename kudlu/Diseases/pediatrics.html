Pediatrics

Paediatrics is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to infants, children, and adolescents. It covers disorders such as infections, growth issues, congenital diseases, and childhood immunization.

Paediatricians treat conditions such as respiratory infections, nutritional deficiencies, allergies, and chronic childhood diseases. They also provide essential vaccinations, developmental assessments, and preventive healthcare services.

Early diagnosis and proper healthcare are crucial for ensuring a child's healthy development. Regular pediatric check-ups and timely vaccinations can significantly improve long-term well-being.

Symptoms
  • Fever & frequent infections
  • Delayed growth & development
  • Allergies & breathing difficulties
  • Poor appetite & weight loss
  • Skin rashes & irritations
  • Behavioral or sleep issues

  • Nutritional deficiencies
