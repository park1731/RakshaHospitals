Orthopedics

Orthopedics is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to bones, joints, muscles, and the musculoskeletal system. It covers disorders such as fractures, arthritis, spinal conditions, and sports injuries.

Orthopedic specialists treat conditions such as joint pain, osteoporosis, ligament injuries, and congenital musculoskeletal disorders. They also perform procedures like joint replacement, arthroscopy, and spinal surgeries.

Early diagnosis and proper orthopedic care are crucial for maintaining mobility and quality of life. Regular check-ups and timely treatment can significantly improve patient outcomes.

Comprehensive Orthopedic Treatments

Our orthopedics department offers personalized treatment plans that include:

  • ✔ Medications for pain and inflammation
  • ✔ Minimally invasive procedures for joint and spine conditions
  • ✔ Physiotherapy & rehabilitation for mobility improvement
  • ✔ Joint replacement surgeries for arthritis and injuries
  • ✔ Advanced diagnostic imaging for bone and joint assessment
  • ✔ Customized orthopedic support & bracing for posture correction

Musculoskeletal conditions can significantly impact daily life and mobility. At Raksha Hospital, we focus on early diagnosis, advanced treatments, and rehabilitation to help patients regain their strength and function.
