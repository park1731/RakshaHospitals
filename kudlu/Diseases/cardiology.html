Cardiology

Cardiology is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating conditions related to the heart and circulatory system. It covers disorders such as heart disease, hypertension, arrhythmias, and heart failure.

Cardiologists treat conditions such as coronary artery disease, congenital heart defects, valvular disorders, and high blood pressure. They also perform procedures like angioplasty, pacemaker implantation, and cardiac surgery.

Early diagnosis and proper heart care are crucial for maintaining cardiovascular health. Regular check-ups and timely intervention can significantly improve patient outcomes.

Comprehensive Cardiology Treatments

Our cardiology department offers personalized treatment plans that include:

  • ✔ Medications for heart disease and blood pressure control
  • ✔ Minimally invasive procedures for heart conditions
  • ✔ Cardiac catheterization for diagnostic and treatment purposes
  • ✔ Advanced imaging & stress testing for heart function assessment
  • ✔ Heart surgery including valve replacement & bypass surgery
  • ✔ Rehabilitation & lifestyle counseling for long-term heart health

Heart disease is one of the leading causes of health complications. At Raksha Hospital, we focus on early detection, advanced treatment, and preventive heart care to help patients lead a healthy and active life.

Symptoms
  • Chest pain & discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Fatigue & dizziness
  • Swelling in legs & feet
  • High blood pressure
Causes
  • High cholesterol & hypertension
  • Diabetes & obesity
  • Smoking & excessive alcohol
  • Genetic predisposition
  • Poor diet & sedentary lifestyle
  • Stress & mental health factors
Treatment
  • Medications for heart health
  • Angioplasty & stent placement
  • Pacemakers & defibrillators
  • Heart surgery & bypass procedures
  • Cardiac rehabilitation programs
  • Lifestyle modifications
Care
  • Regular cardiac check-ups
  • Maintaining a heart-healthy diet
  • Regular physical activity
  • Managing stress & mental well-being
  • Monitoring blood pressure & cholesterol
  • Avoiding smoking & excessive alcohol
