Oncology

Oncology is a specialized branch of medicine that focuses on diagnosing and treating various types of cancer. It includes medical, surgical, and radiation oncology to provide comprehensive cancer care and management.

Oncologists treat conditions such as breast cancer, lung cancer, leukemia, and lymphoma. They provide chemotherapy, radiation therapy, immunotherapy, and targeted treatments to help patients fight cancer effectively.

Early detection, personalized treatment plans, and advanced therapies are crucial for improving cancer survival rates. Comprehensive cancer care enhances the quality of life for patients.

Why Raksha?

Our Oncology team at Raksha Hospital comprises highly skilled specialists experienced in diagnosing and treating various cancers. At Raksha, we provide expert care in:

  • Comprehensive Cancer Diagnosis & Staging
  • Medical Oncology (Chemotherapy, Immunotherapy)
  • Radiation Therapy & Targeted Treatments
  • Surgical Oncology & Tumor Removal
  • Palliative & Supportive Cancer Care
  • Genetic Counseling & Cancer Prevention

Comprehensive Oncology Treatments

Our oncology department offers personalized treatment plans that include:

  • ✔ Medications for chemotherapy & targeted therapies
  • ✔ Advanced imaging & biopsy for accurate diagnosis
  • ✔ Minimally invasive cancer surgeries
  • ✔ Radiation therapy for tumor reduction
  • ✔ Immunotherapy & hormone therapy for cancer control
  • ✔ Psychological & nutritional support for holistic care

Cancer treatment requires a multidisciplinary approach and continuous monitoring. At Raksha Hospital, we focus on early diagnosis, cutting-edge treatments, and compassionate care to help patients fight cancer and improve their quality of life.

Symptoms
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Chronic fatigue
  • Persistent pain
  • Unusual lumps or swelling
  • Changes in skin or moles
  • Difficulty swallowing or persistent cough
Causes
  • Genetic mutations
  • Smoking & alcohol consumption
  • Exposure to radiation & carcinogens
  • Unhealthy diet & obesity
  • Chronic infections (HPV, Hepatitis B/C)
  • Family history of cancer
Treatment
  • Chemotherapy & radiation therapy
  • Targeted therapy & immunotherapy
  • Minimally invasive cancer surgery
  • Bone marrow transplants
  • Hormonal therapy for certain cancers
  • Palliative care & pain management
Care
  • Regular cancer screenings
  • Healthy lifestyle & balanced diet
  • Managing side effects of treatments
  • Emotional & psychological support
  • Routine follow-up & monitoring
  • Early detection & prevention strategies
